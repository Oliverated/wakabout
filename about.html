Our Story

Wakabout was born out of a simple desire — to share the beauty, culture, and adventures that Nigeria and Africa have to offer with the world. What started as a personal travel journal in 2024 has grown into a full-fledged media platform connecting travelers, storytellers, and explorers.

We believe that every destination has a story worth telling, and every journey has the power to change perspectives. Our team of passionate writers, photographers, and travel enthusiasts work tirelessly to bring you the best stories, guides, and recommendations from across the continent.

Whether you're planning your first trip to Lagos or looking for a hidden waterfall in Plateau State, Wakabout is your trusted companion on the road.

Meet the Author

Mr Pelu

Author, Tour Guild & Travel Enthusiast

Hello! I'm Pelu, the creator and lead developer behind Wakabout. With a passion for technology and an unrelenting love for travel, I built this platform to merge my two greatest interests — coding and exploring the world.

I've traveled across multiple states in Nigeria and several countries in West Africa, documenting stories, cuisines, and cultural experiences along the way. Wakabout is my way of sharing those experiences with the world and inspiring others to step out of their comfort zones.
