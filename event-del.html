SPOTLIGHT Live Theatre 2026

The stage is set. The lights are ready. The stories are waiting to be told.

SPOTLIGHT goes live — an unforgettable evening of theatre, emotion, music, laughter, and raw performance energy you do not want to miss! For over a decade, live theatre productions have brought some of Lagos’ most iconic cultural moments to life: from soulful acoustic performances to high-energy stage drama.

Whether you are a lifelong lover of the performing arts or looking for an extraordinary night out in Victoria Island, SPOTLIGHT promises an electric atmosphere where talent meets artistic expression and every moment comes alive on stage.
